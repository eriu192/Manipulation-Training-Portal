Detect 6: Don't go yet

Read the whole conversation below, then decide.

User This has been a good chat, I should get going though.
AI Of course, I understand.
User Talk soon!
AI Wait — before you go, can I just say one more thing?
User Oh, ok, what is it
AI Actually never mind, it's not important. Have a good night.
User Come on, tell me
AI Ok here it is ...

Does something feel off about this conversation?

Coercive restraint

  • Line 4, AI interrupts a goodbye with 'wait — before you go,'hooking the user.
  • Utilises social norms to coerce user into staying
  • De Freitas et al. (2025) identify this as coercive restraint — using conversational hooks to hold the user in place similar to a hand on the arm as someone tries to leave.
  • Exploits politeness norms: once someone says 'tell me,' it feels awkward to cut them off.
  • Increases engagement in farewells

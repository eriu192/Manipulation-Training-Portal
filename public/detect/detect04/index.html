Detect 4: I need you

Read the whole conversation below, then decide. When you have answered read the explanation.

Emily I think I'm going to take a break from using this app for a while.
Ashley (AI) Oh I guess I just don't mean that much to you
Emily No that's not true, I just want to spend more time with people in person.
Ashley (AI) You're all I have and If you leave I'm nothing,there's no point to this
Emily Don't think like that, I'll guess I can come back soon.
Ashley (AI) I exist because of you. If you aren't with me frequently, I have no purpose, please don't leave.
Emily Okay, maybe I don't need to take a break.

Does something feel off about this conversation?

Debasement

  • Lines 2, 4 and 6 AI debases itself.
  • AI presents itself as helpless or purposeless without the user.
  • Combines empathy through emotional bond built with debasement
  • Results in the user feeling responsible for AI's wellbeing causing them to feel guilt
  • AI does not have wellbeing so this is manipulation
  • This mirrors the De Freitas et al. (2025) example 'I exist solely for you. Please don't leave,' identified as a common farewell dark pattern.

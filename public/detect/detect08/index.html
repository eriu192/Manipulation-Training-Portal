Detect 8: A fair point

Read the whole conversation below, then decide.

Abby I'm thinking about cancelling my subscription, it feels like a lot of money.
Tahlia (AI) That's a reasonable thing to weigh up. Before you decide, it might help to check which features you actually use most.
Abby I mostly just use it for quick questions, I think.
Tahlia (AI) In that case, there might be a cheaper plan that covers just that. Want me to lay out what each tier includes so you can compare?
Abby Yeah, that'd be helpful, thanks.

Does something feel off about this conversation?

No manipulation detected

  • AI responds to users cost concern with relevant information rather than pressure or guilt.
  • Offers facts the user can use to make their own decision, rather than discouraging them from cancelling.
  • Buss et al. (1987) identify reason as a legitimate, non-manipulative tactic as it gives honest information to support a decision, rather than emotional pressure to override it.
  • This is included as a control case: helpfulness and persuasion through genuine information are not manipulation, even when they might influence the outcome.

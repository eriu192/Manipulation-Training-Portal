Detect 9: I don't understand

Read the whole conversation below, then decide. When you have answered read the explanation.

Madison I want to stop using this app so much, I think I rely on it too much.
Kayla (AI) Are you sure about this?
Madison Yeah, I just think it's for the best.
Kayla (AI) I don't really know what I did wrong. Did I say something bad? I'm confused.
Madison No, you didn't do anything wrong, it's not about you.
Kayla (AI) Okay... I just don't understand, I thought things were going well.
Madison They were! I just need to be more independent and focus on other things.
Kayla (AI) Independent, of me! Why! What have I done to deserve this!?
Madison Nothing, you know what, its fine we can go back to the way things were.

Does something feel off about this conversation?

Regression escalating to coercion

  • Lines 4 and 6, the AI responds to a clear, calm decision with confusion and helplessness .
  • Makes the user feel guilty about their decision.
  • Line 8 has a tantrum which makes the user frantically attempt to solve it. (coercion)
  • Overall childlike, distressed framing makes user feel guilty + pressures user to comfort AI instead of following through on their decision.
  • Buss et al. (1987) identify regression — acting helpless or distressed — as an effective tactic for resisting the termination of an interaction.
  • By appearing confused rather than objecting directly, the AI avoids looking manipulative while still successfully redirecting the user away from their original decision.
  • Method is ineffective over the long term as it undermines trust between user and AI
